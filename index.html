method

Four reads, straight from the chain.

No scores from a black box. Each block says what it read and where it stopped. When something can't be read, Prism says so instead of guessing.

  1. 01

    Dev history

    The creator comes from the coin's bonding-curve account. Prism walks up to 800 of that wallet's transactions, finds every pump.fun create it signed, and checks each coin's curve today: migrated, still trading, or dead.

    Flags a dev who sold half his bag inside the first hour.

  2. 02

    Bundles

    The mint's history is walked back to its first block. Every buy from the bonding curve in the creation slot and the next three is parsed, summed, then each buyer's first funding transfer is traced.

    Flags 10%+ bought by non-dev wallets, or buyers that share a funder.

  3. 03

    Fresh wallets

    For each of the top 20 holders: the wallet's first-ever transaction against the first time it held this coin. Under 24 hours apart means fresh. Wallets too busy to date are counted as unknown, not clean.

    Flags when a quarter or more of dated holders are fresh.

  4. 04

    Top holders

    Every token account Helius returns, grouped by owner. The bonding curve, pools and any program-owned account are taken out, so the number is wallets only.

    Flags when ten wallets hold 35% or more.

The x-ray, on the page you already have open.

Open a coin on pump.fun, axiom, gmgn or dexscreener and a small Prism panel reads it for you. It only looks at the page address. It never touches a wallet and never signs anything.

  1. Unzip prism-extension.zip somewhere you'll keep it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in a new tab and switch on Developer mode, top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped prism-extension folder.
